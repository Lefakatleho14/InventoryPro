Everything You Need

A complete inventory solution built specifically for small retail businesses

📦
Product Management
Add, edit and delete products with full CRUD operations. Search and filter your entire catalogue instantly.
🛒
Sales Recording
Record sales with automatic stock deduction. Generate printable receipts for every transaction.
⚠️
Low Stock Alerts
Automatic alerts when products fall below their reorder level. Never run out of stock unexpectedly.
📊
Reports & Analytics
Sales reports, inventory valuation and low stock reports — all exportable to PDF and CSV.
🔐
Role-Based Access
Manager and Shop Assistant roles with different permissions. Full access control throughout the system.
📱
Android Mobile App
Available on Google Play Store. Manage inventory on the go with the same features as the web app.

Built for Your Team

Two roles, clearly defined permissions

Manager

  • ✅ Full product management
  • ✅ View all reports
  • ✅ Export PDF and CSV
  • ✅ Record and view sales
  • ✅ Dashboard with full stats

Shop Assistant

  • ✅ View product catalogue
  • ✅ Record sales
  • ✅ View sales history
  • ✅ View dashboard
  • ❌ No access to reports
