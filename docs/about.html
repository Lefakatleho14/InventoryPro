About InventoryPro

Learn about the system, the team and the client we built it for.

The System

Built to solve a real business problem

The Problem

Durban Small Business Supplies was managing their entire inventory using paper records and spreadsheets. This led to stock discrepancies, no real-time visibility, and no reporting capability.

Our Solution

InventoryPro is a full-stack inventory management system that automates stock tracking, sales recording, and management reporting — available on both web and Android mobile.

❌ No real-time stock tracking
❌ Manual sales recording only
❌ No reporting capability
❌ No role-based access control

The Client

🏪

Durban Small Business Supplies

A small retail business based in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. They supply stationery, office equipment and business essentials to local businesses and individuals.
